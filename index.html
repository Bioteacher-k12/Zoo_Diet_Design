Dietary Planning: Critical Information

Different species can have very specific needs. When feeding animals, whether at the zoo or your own pet at home, it is vital to provide a diet that is biologically appropriate.

  1. Formulate a biologically appropriate diet that meets or exceeds each species' minimum needs.
  2. Each species should be offered a minimum of three (3) food items daily.
  3. To calculate the mass of total portion to be fed each day, multiply the portion size by the percentage of each macromolecule category (protein, fat, carbohydrate). Convert percentages to decimals first (e.g., 8.9% = 0.089).
  4. If struggling to keep within the recommended range for your species, consider reducing the portion size, which will not have an impact on its macromolecule percentages.
